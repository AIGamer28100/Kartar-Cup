## Two accounts, two roles
- **@thekartercup** — "The Karter Cup". 90 posts, 980 followers, 71 following. Bio: "Motorsport Events & Community / Karting events | Sim Racing | Watch Party's / Watch parties → @thekarterclub". Links: linktr.ee/teamkartercup, Threads @thekartercup. This is the main brand — karting league + community.
- **@thekarterclub** — "The Karter Club". 27 posts, 9 followers, 1 following (follows @thekartercup). Bio: "Chennai's Official F1 Paddock Community 🏎️🏁 / watchparties | sim racing | community / An Initiative by @thekartercup". **This is the real, dedicated watch-party sub-brand** — the closest existing analog to what Kartar CUP (our site) is for.

## Real logo (both, described — not downloaded)
- **Karter Cup mark**: circular badge, black fill, checkered-flag ring border, a red→orange gradient inner accent ring, a stylised go-kart/racer silhouette in white/red at centre, "KARTER" arched across the top in white, "CUP" arched across the bottom in white.
- **Karter Club mark**: circular badge, black/gold palette (vs Karter Cup's black/red-orange), checkered-flag ring, "KARTER" top / "CLUB" bottom.
- Palette read from the marks: black base, checkered white/black ring, a red-to-orange gradient as the accent (not a flat red) — closer to our current fallback red than a fully different hue, but a gradient, not flat. Karter Club's variant leans gold/amber instead of red-orange.

## C) Analysis

Price ladder. Karter Cup Monza at 399 sits between the Bengaluru cafe entry (299) and the Chennai cafe (650 onwards), below pub 500. Its inclusion list (paddock pass, driver cards, snacks, games) is richer than comparables, so 399 is a competitive ceiling for a snacks-included event. Group ladder observed: 399 / 798 / 1,596, exactly linear.

Discounts. Only structure observed: group and duo bundles priced linearly (no saving). Grid uses strike-through (699 -> 599, about 14% off). Free/RSVP at rooftop and cafe events. No early-bird, coupon, student or couple discounts found on any fetched page.

Fees. District/Urbanaut page data shows a flat convenience fee of 75.00 and 5.00% on Urbanaut (unconfirmed which applies to buyers). Grid shows "Inc taxes" price display. Fee patterns for other platforms: not found.

Refund norms. Non-refundable is the norm found (District: no refund even on rescheduling; HighApe: no exchange/refund). Exception: Fosite fee described as "refundable fee" (deposit style, redeemed at entry). Age: 18+/21+ at alcohol venues; all ages at cafe; karting needs height/age limits.

UX patterns worth copying: stated inclusions list (paddock pass, keychain, sim coupon); ID/age line up front; "space limited" scarcity cue; single/duo/group selector; strike-through price; clear no-refund text before payment; per-event dress code/theme line.

Suggested defaults (assumptions, not findings; host to confirm): cafe/pub watch party 399-499 with snacks, 299 entry-only with merch; premium venue 599-699; group of 4 at 5-10% off (currently 0); early-bird 10-15% for the first 20% of seats; karting event separate SKU (ticket price unverified).

## D) Data model for admin-managed pricing

- Venue: id, name, type (cafe|pub|cinema|karting_track|event_space|other), city, address, map_url, capacity_default, age_min, active.
- Event: id, title, kind (watch_party|karting|sim), starts_at, ends_at, venue_id, status (draft|on_sale|sold_out|ended), sales_open_at, sales_close_at, capacity_total, age_min, terms_md, refund_policy (none|full_until|partial), refund_cutoff_hours.
- Tier: id, event_id, name (Single/Duo/Group of 4), party_size, base_price_paise, includes[], capacity, per_order_max, sort, active.
- VenuePriceOverride: tier_template_id or tier_id, venue_id, price_paise, valid_from, valid_to (override beats event/tier base).
- Discount: id, code (nullable), name, type (percent|flat|bogo|group|early_bird|coupon|student|couple), value, min_qty, group_size, applies_to (all|event_ids|venue_ids|tier_ids), valid_from, valid_to, usage_limit_total, usage_limit_per_user, used_count, stackable (bool), priority, requires_proof, active.
- FeeRule: id, kind (convenience|platform|gst), mode (flat|percent), value, applies_to, included_in_price (bool), payer (buyer|host), gst_rate.
- Booking/Order: id, event_id, tier_id, qty, buyer name, phone, email (fields to collect: not found on Karter Cup pages; suggest name, phone, email, party names, optional driver/team name), price_breakdown (base, discounts[], fees, tax, total), payment_status, payment_method, code, checkin_status.
- Capacity: track sold per tier and per event; hold seats during payment (timeout).
- AuditLog: who changed which price when.
- Store money as integer paise, all price computation server-side, snapshot the price breakdown on the order.
